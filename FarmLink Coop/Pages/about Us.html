About Us

FarmLink Cooperative is an agricultural cooperative that brings farmers together to access farming inouts, share knowledge, improve products, and find opportunities in the market.

Our Vision

To become a leading agricultural cooperative taht empowers farmers and contributes to sustainable agricultural development.

Our Missioon

To support farmers by providing access to quality inputs, useful agricultural information, modern farming techniques, and reliable market for thier products.

Our Goal

  • Improve agricultural productivity among farmers.
  • Provide farmers with access to quality seeds and fertilizer.
  • Promote modern and sustainable farming methods.
  • Help farmers access better markets for thier produce.
  • Encourage cooperation and knowledge sharing among farmers.
  • Improve the income and livelihood of farmers.
  • Use technology to make agricultural services easier to access
