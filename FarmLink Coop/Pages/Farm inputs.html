Understanding Farm Inputs

Agricultural inputs are materials and equipment used during farming activities. Selecting appropriate inputs can help farmers manage crops and improve production.

Common Agricultural Inputs

Seeds

Maize, soybean, groundnut, bean, and vegetable seeds suited to local growing conditions.

Fertilizers

Products used to supply nutrients to crops based on soil and crop needs.

Farming Tools

Hoes, watering cans, wheelbarrows, and other equipment for farm work.

Irrigation Equipment

Equipment that helps farmers supply water to crops when needed.

Responsible Input Selection

  • Choose seeds suited to your local climate.
  • Consider soil testing before fertilizing.
  • Follow product labels and safety guidance.
  • Use appropriate protective equipment.
  • Seek qualified agricultural advice.
